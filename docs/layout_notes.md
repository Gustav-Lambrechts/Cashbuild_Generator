# Cashbuild Layout Notes

## Planning Priorities
- Trading Area is the highest priority zone.
- Protect Trading Area first if the layout becomes constrained.
- Keep entrance flow readable and service flow practical.
- Add flexible support zones around the main trading rectangle.

## Key Standards

### Trading Preferred
- Preferred trading size: `30000 x 36000 mm`
- Preferred trading area: `1080 m²`

### Trading Compact
- Compact trading size: `23200 x 35650 mm`

### Service Band
- Service band depth: `6230 mm`

### Admin Block
- Admin target area: `130 m²`
- Typical admin depth range: `6050-6385 mm`

### External Service Zones
- Yard minimum: `900 m²`
- Off-loading minimum: `450 m²`

## Zone Rules

### Trading
- Trading sits behind the entrance.
- Trading remains the dominant zone.
- Trading should stay as a clean, legible rectangle where possible.
- POS belongs on the entrance wall.

### Admin Block
- Admin stays outside Trading.
- Admin is part of the service/admin edge logic.

### Goods Receiving
- Goods Receiving must touch Admin.
- Goods Receiving must touch Trading.
- Goods Receiving should support practical service access.

### Yard
- Yard is external.
- Yard defaults to an L-shape where practical.
- Yard should connect to the Admin / Goods Receiving service cluster.

### Off-loading
- Off-loading sits inside or directly attached to Yard.
- Off-loading supports unloading, storage, and maneuvering.

### Parking
- Parking is flexible.
- Parking can move to support the stronger trading and service relationships.
